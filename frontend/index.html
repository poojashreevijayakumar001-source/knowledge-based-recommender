How does this work without any history?

  1. Constraints: your budget and brand choices remove products that can't work.
  2. Domain knowledge: rules translate a need (e.g. "photography") into specs (e.g. 50MP+ camera).
  3. Scoring: each product is scored on how many weighted rules it satisfies, plus rating and popularity.
  4. Explanation: every result tells you why it was picked and what it lacks.
  5. Relaxation: if nothing fits, constraints are loosened step by step and you are told what changed.
